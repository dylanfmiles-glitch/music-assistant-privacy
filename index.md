# Privacy Notice — Music Assistant Alexa

**Last updated:** October 27, 2026

## Overview

Music Assistant Alexa is a personal, noncommercial integration intended to connect my Amazon Echo devices with my self-hosted Music Assistant server.

It is not affiliated with Amazon or Spotify.

## Information Used

The integration may process Amazon account authorization information, device identifiers, playback commands, and media metadata necessary to control music playback.

## How Information Is Used

Information is used solely to authenticate my Amazon account, communicate with my Echo devices, and enable music playback through Music Assistant.

The integration is not intended for public use, advertising, analytics, or the sale of personal information.

## Storage and Third Parties

Application configuration and authentication information may be stored on my self-hosted systems. Amazon, Spotify, and other connected services process information according to their own privacy policies.

The integration does not intentionally disclose personal information to unrelated third parties.

## Security

Access to the integration is protected using authentication and encrypted HTTPS connections. No internet-connected service can be guaranteed completely secure.

## Data Removal

Authorization can be revoked through Amazon account settings. Locally stored integration data can be removed by deleting the relevant application configuration.

## Contact

This application is intended solely for personal use and is not offered as a public service.
